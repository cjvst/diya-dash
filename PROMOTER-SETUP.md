# Diya Dash — promoter setup (one page)

**Link:** https://cjvst.github.io/diya-dash/

## Before the shift (5 minutes, on Wi-Fi)
1. **Open the link once on Wi-Fi.** After that it works even with no signal at the stall.
2. **Add it to the home screen.**
   - **Android (Chrome):** menu ⋮ → *Add to Home screen*.
   - **iPhone (Safari):** Share → *Add to Home Screen*. This hides the browser bars; iPhones can't go full screen otherwise.
3. **Phone settings:**
   - Brightness at maximum.
   - Volume up, silent mode off.
   - Auto-rotate off. The game is played upright.
4. **Set your stall code.** Press and hold the **TOTAL logo** on the start screen for about a second to open **Promoter tools**. Type your stall code (for example `KOL-07`) and tap **Done**. The code appears on every result screen.
5. **Daily prize limits (optional).** In Promoter tools, enter how many of each prize you can give today. When one runs out, players who earn it get the next prize down. Leave a limit blank for no limit.

## Running the game
- Hand the phone over on the start screen. The player taps **Let's GO!**, plays 30 seconds, and sees the result.
- **Checking a win:**
  - The result screen shows a **live clock** (it keeps ticking, and the green dot pulses), your stall code and a result code such as `DD-7K4Q`.
  - A screenshot can't be passed off as a new win: its clock has stopped.
- **When you hand a prize over,** tap **Prize given**. The card is stamped with the time, and today's counts stay right.
- Tap **Next player** for the next person.
- **"Best TOTAL today" on the start screen** gives the queue a score to beat.

## End of the shift
- Open **Promoter tools** (hold the TOTAL logo). It shows:
  - today's plays;
  - each prize won and how many were handed over;
  - the average score and the best score.
- Tap **Export results** to send the day's list, with times, scores, prizes and codes, on WhatsApp or by email. If the phone can't share files, it saves a CSV instead.
- **Clear today** wipes today's results on this phone. Only use it if told to.

## Good to know
- **Charging:** the phone rests itself on the start screen and on the result screen, so it stays cooler. Keep it on charge between rushes anyway.
- **Vibration:** works on Android phones. iPhones don't vibrate from a web page.
- **Results stay on this phone only.** Nothing about the player is collected.
